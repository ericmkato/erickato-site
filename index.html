SE playbook

practices from 20 years of technical selling
discovery

Earn the demo

No demo before discovery. Know the business pain, the people who feel it, and what success looks like in their numbers.

demo

Show three things, not thirty

Open with the outcome, show the few workflows that solve their stated pain, and stop. Every extra feature is a new objection.

poc

No POC without exit criteria

Agree on written success criteria, a timeline and a decision date before any proof of concept starts.

metrics

Measure SE impact

Track technical win rate, stage conversion and SE attribution so the team's value shows up in the forecast, not just in anecdotes.

enablement

Make the playbook reusable

Turn what top SEs do into demo scripts, discovery guides and reference architectures the whole team can use.

ai

Put AI in the deal, not the slide

Agents that prep account research, draft follow-ups and coach on discovery free SEs for the work only people can do.
